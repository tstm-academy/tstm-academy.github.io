RSL Acoustic Guitar: Debut Grade Portal

⚠️ Mandatory Recording Guidelines

  • Continuous Take: Single, continuous recording in real-time. No video editing, cutting, or post-production mixing is permitted. Leave re-tuning/amp adjustment in the shot.
  • Video Specs: Min 720p, Max 1080p, Frame Rate 24–30 fps. File size limit is 2GB.
  • Framing: Full extent of the student, instrument, and equipment (pedals/amps) must be completely visible.
  • Audio Balance: Backing tracks must be clearly audible directly from the camera microphone audio.

Official Exam Video Script (Candidate Only)

Part 1: Introduction

Candidate: "Hello. My name is [Candidate's Name]. Today is [Recording Date], and I am appearing for the Rockschool Acoustic Guitar Debut Grade Exam."

*(Candidate holds up the device showing the digital book or purchase confirmation clearly to the camera for a few seconds)*

Candidate: "Here is my official RSL digital grade book resource for this exam."

Part 2: Performance Pieces

Candidate: "First, I will play the performance pieces. My first performance is Knockin' on Heaven's Door."

*(Plays piece 1 with backing track without click. Brief pause to reset)*

Candidate: "My second performance is Ain't No Sunshine."

*(Plays piece 2 with backing track without click. Brief pause to reset)*

Candidate: "My third performance is I'm Yours."

*(Plays piece 3 with backing track without click. Brief pause to reset)*

Part 3: Technical Exercises

Candidate: "Next, I will play the technical exercises. I will start with Group A, scales, played with a backing track. First is the C Major Scale."

*(Plays scale)*

Candidate: "Next is the E Minor Pentatonic Scale."

*(Plays scale)*

Candidate: "Next is the A Minor Pentatonic Scale."

*(Plays scale)*

Candidate: "Now I will play Group B, chords. The chords are A, D, C, G, A minor, and E minor."

*(Plays chord progression)*

Candidate: "Finally, I will play Group C, the Acoustic Riff, with a backing track."

*(Plays riff with backing track)*

Part 4: Conclusion

Candidate: "Thank you. That concludes my exam."

*(Let camera run for 2 seconds before cutting)*
